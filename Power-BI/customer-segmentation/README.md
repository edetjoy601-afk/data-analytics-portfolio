
# Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Overview
This project uses Power BI to explore customer characteristics and develop an interactive view of customer demographics and distribution.

## Objectives
- Examine customer demographics.
- Summarize income and customer tenure.
- Compare customer distribution across regions.
- Present key metrics in a dashboard.

## Tools
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query, where applicable
- DAX measures
- Data visualization

## Dashboard Metrics
- Customer count: approximately 500
- Average income: 114.40k
- Average tenure: 5.96 years
- Average age: 46.28 years

## Key Insights
- Customer distribution is relatively balanced across gender, with a slight majority of female customers
- The South region recorded the highest number of customers, while the West had the lowest
- Senior customers and adults make up the largest portion of the customer base
- Retail customers dominate compared to corporate and private segment
- Engineers and teachers represent a significant portion of the customer population


## Analysis
The report explores customer distribution by region and gender, alongside demographic and tenure metrics.
