
# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis

## Overview
This project uses SQL to explore retail sales data and evaluate sales and profit performance across regions and product categories.

## Objectives
- Explore the structure and quality of the data.
- Calculate total sales and profit.
- Compare performance across regions.
- Examine product category performance.
- Use SQL to answer business questions.

## Tools
- SQL
- SQL queries
- Aggregations and grouping
- Filtering and sorting

## Methodology
1. Inspected the dataset and its columns.
2. Checked records and relevant fields.
3. Used aggregate functions to calculate sales and profit.
4. Grouped results by region and product category.
5. Reviewed query results for consistency.

## Key Results
- Total sales: 2,296,919.48
- Total profit: 286,409.85
- Highest-performing region by sales: West
- Leading product category by sales: Technology

## Key Findings
- Overall sales and profit generated across all orders.
- Identified the region with the highest sales performance.
- Evaluated how different product categories contribute to both revenue and profitability.
- While some categories generated high sales, they do not necessarily yield the highest profit, highlighting differences in profit margins across products.
- Variations in purchasing behavior, particularly in terms of average order value.
- Identified the most profitable product subcategories, providing deeper insight into the key drivers of business performance.

- Results and screenshots

SQL querying, aggregation, exploratory analysis, and business reporting.
